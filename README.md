## A little about me

I build websites and web applications that connect a strong first impression
with the systems a business needs behind it.

My work spans real estate platforms, custom dashboards, business management
tools, and brand websites. I care about the details on both sides:
how an interface feels to a customer, and how easily a team can manage it
after launch.

At **Code Folks**, I lead development and work with a small team across
design, content, and delivery. I'm also a computer science student at
**IMSciences**, a musician, and a photographer—interests that shape my
approach to composition, rhythm, and visual storytelling.

**My focus:** bring the interface, application logic, and data together
into a product people can actually use.

## How I approach a build

- **Understand the workflow.** Start with the people, tasks, and business problem.
- **Design with intent.** Make navigation, typography, and interactions serve the experience.
- **Connect the whole system.** Treat the frontend, backend, and database as one product.
- **Make it manageable.** Give teams practical ways to update content and run operations.
- **Refine the details.** Pay attention to responsiveness, loading states, and everyday usability.
